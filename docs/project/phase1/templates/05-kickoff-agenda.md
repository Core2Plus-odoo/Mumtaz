## Presentation outline

1. **Title** — client name, project name, date, C2P Consultants.
2. **The outcome** — one slide, the client's goal in their words.
3. **Team** — faces, names, roles, who to contact for what.
4. **Scope** — in, and explicitly out. Give the exclusions equal weight.
5. **Deliverables** — each with its acceptance method and named approver.
6. **Plan** — milestones against dates, with assumptions visible on the slide.
7. **What we need from you** — the workbook tabs, owners, dates.
8. **How acceptance works** — reached, offered, accepted; and that billing
   follows acceptance, not activity.
9. **Governance** — meeting rhythm, reporting, escalation path.
10. **Risks** — the top three, with owners.
11. **Next 14 days** — a short dated list.
12. **Questions.**
