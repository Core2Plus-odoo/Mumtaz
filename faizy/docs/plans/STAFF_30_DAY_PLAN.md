# FAIZY staff 30-day work plan

Generated by `faizy/odoo/tools/provision_staff.py` from `staff_plan_30d.py`. Start: 2026-11-24. Six working days a week, 8 productive hours a day (6.5 h routine + 1.5 h project block). Every task needs evidence before Done. No prices or claims anywhere.

## SOPs

### SOP-C1 Inbound WhatsApp triage

1. Open Care Desk › Inbound to handle. Work oldest first.
2. Identify the customer and the family member; search the partner before creating one.
3. Answer through a reply draft (the AI drafts, you approve) or create an order draft for Operations.
4. Mark each message Handled. Ignore only spam, and say why in a note.
5. Anything about a complaint, refund or safety goes to Umer as an escalation draft; never answer those alone.
6. Evidence: a note on the task with the counts (handled, order drafts created, escalated).

### SOP-C2 Reply approval and manual send

1. Open Care Desk › Replies awaiting approval.
2. Check against the knowledge notes: facts, tone, no stale phone number, no prices unless approved.
3. Approve, or reject with the reason the agent should learn from.
4. While sending is off: send the approved text from the official WhatsApp phone, then click "Mark as sent manually".
5. Only inside 10:00 to 21:00 Karachi.
6. Evidence: counts approved, rejected and sent by hand, in a note on the task.

### SOP-C3 Completion message and follow-up

1. Open Care Desk › My work today › Completion message items.
2. Confirm Operations approved the proof and receipt.
3. Tell the customer what was done, when, with the photo; ask if anything else is needed.
4. Three days later, a short check-in; note the answer on the order.
5. Evidence: note with the orders messaged and any follow-up booked.

### SOP-C4 Social post of the day (FaizyHaiNa)

1. One post a day on the Facebook page, one story on Instagram, from the content calendar.
2. Allowed content: what Faizy does, how it works, a Faizy at work (with consent), a customer story (with consent).
3. Not allowed without written approval: prices, plan numbers, customer counts, follower counts, "free".
4. Reply to every comment and message on the post within the send window.
5. Evidence: the post link or a screenshot attached to the task.

### SOP-C5 Digital lead qualification

1. Open Care Desk › My leads. New leads from Facebook, Instagram, the website and WhatsApp land on you.
2. Contact inside 10:00 to 21:00 Karachi. Confirm family city, expat country, the service they need and the family size.
3. Fill the Faizy tab on the lead. Move to Qualified when the need and the city are confirmed; mark Lost with the reason otherwise.
4. A qualified lead gets a follow-up the next day: plan options that fit, no prices until approved.
5. Won: create the customer and the first order draft. Hand operational questions to Raza through an order draft.
6. Evidence: the lead records themselves plus a note with counts (contacted, qualified, lost).

### SOP-O1 Morning operations review and assignment

1. Open Operations Desk › Unassigned orders, Jobs not started, Order drafts.
2. For each unassigned order: pick a Faizy or vendor active in that city and service, confirm the slot on WhatsApp, click Assign.
3. Urgent orders first (escalation is 1 h), then by creation time.
4. Jobs not started 1 h after their slot: call the Faizy, reschedule or reassign, tell care if the customer must know.
5. Evidence: note with counts (assigned, rescheduled, still open and why).

### SOP-O2 Proof and receipt review

1. Open Operations Desk › Proof & receipt review.
2. The photo must show the job done for the right family member; the receipt must match the purchase value.
3. Approve the evidence, or reject with what must be redone. The customer sees proof only after approval.
4. Completion without approved evidence is refused by the system; do not ask for exceptions.
5. Evidence: note with counts (approved, rejected and why).

### SOP-O3 Faizy recruitment and application review

1. Open Operations Desk › Applications.
2. CNIC and photo legible and matching; two references called; city and services recorded.
3. Approve or reject within 7 days of the application.
4. Onboarding: documents on file, the service briefing done, the first job shadowed.
5. Evidence: application records plus a note with calls made.

### SOP-O4 Partner outreach (pharmacies, labs, clinics, embassies)

1. Five new contacts a day from the partner target list, by call or WhatsApp.
2. Create each as a lead with source Embassy Programme, Walk-in / Phone or Other, so it lands in your pipeline.
3. Say what Faizy does and what a partnership looks like; never quote prices or commissions until approved.
4. Book a visit or call for interested partners; write the next step and date on the lead.
5. Evidence: the lead records plus a note with counts (contacted, interested, visits booked).

### SOP-O5 Partner and referral lead qualification

1. Open Operations Desk › My leads. Partner, embassy and walk-in leads land on you.
2. Qualified means: a named contact, a real need or referral flow, and a next step agreed.
3. Move to Qualified or mark Lost with the reason. Follow-ups are due the next day.
4. Any agreement draft goes to Umer for approval before it is sent.
5. Evidence: the lead records plus a note with counts.

### SOP-X1 End-of-day log and evidence rule

1. Open My desk (KPIs). Read your waiting, overdue and done numbers.
2. Every planned task is marked Done only with evidence on it: a note with what was done and the counts, or an attachment.
3. Anything overdue: write why on the task, then either finish it or ask Umer to reschedule it.
4. Log one note on today's end-of-day task: the three numbers that mattered and one thing to fix tomorrow.
