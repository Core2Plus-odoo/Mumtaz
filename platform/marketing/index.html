Questions

What is Mumtaz built on?

Odoo 19 Community, an open-source business system, with our own modules on top for the Gulf and Pakistan.

Is my data kept apart from other companies?

Yes. Every company gets its own database.

Which countries' invoices do you cover?

The UAE, Saudi Arabia and Pakistan. Tell us which you need and we will show you the format before you start.

Does it work in Arabic?

Yes. Screens and printed documents are available in Arabic and English.

Can you move my existing data?

Yes, as part of an Enterprise setup. Send us a note about what system you use today.
