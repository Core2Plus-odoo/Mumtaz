A day in the boutique

From the morning rate to the evening reconciliation

The rate is approved

The manager enters today's rate; a second person approves it. Until then, nothing prices.

The client is recognised

Her file opens on the counter: preferences, usual spend, the wedding in 21 days, and what suits her — with reasons.

The sale is one transaction

Piece, old-gold credit, discount within limit, AML check, payment, invoice: committed together or not at all.

The grams are reconciled

Each store closes on fine grams, transfers are two-sided, and the owner's cards say what needs a decision tomorrow.

What never changes

Three rules the software enforces, whatever anyone types

One approved rate

The rate that prices a piece was entered by one person and approved by another. The rate used is written on every quote, order and invoice.

A person sends, a person approves

Agents draft messages and recommendations. Sending to a client, moving money or changing a price is a human action with a name on it.

Private means private

Restricted client records are invisible to staff who are not named on them — not greyed out, absent — and every opening is logged.
