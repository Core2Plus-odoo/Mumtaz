The daily loop

Rate to price to sale to old gold to reconciliation

Not an ERP you grow into. The five steps a counter and an owner repeat every day, in order, with the controls a gold business needs.

Today's rate, approved by a second person

One person enters the 24K rate per gram; a different manager approves it. Until they do, prices stay on the previous approved rate. The person who entered a rate cannot approve their own.

Price a piece from that rate

Karat or fineness, wastage, making charge as a percentage, per gram or fixed, stones and VAT. Per gram, per tola or per ounce. A dealer's discount applies to the making charge only, never to the metal.

Sell the piece, not a product line

Each piece is a serial-tracked item with its own gross and net weight, karat, RFID tag and certificate reference. You sell that piece, and you know which one left.

Take old gold in exchange

Gross weight and karat in, melt loss and buy-back margin applied, valued at the approved rate and the rate used saved on the record. Above a limit you set, a second person must approve the payout.

Reconcile and tell the owner

Metal held, stock value at today's rate, exchanges waiting for approval and pieces out on approval, on one screen of decisions rather than a report to go hunting through.

What it covers

Built for how gold is actually sold

Gold rate control

Maker-checker on every rate, the approver and time recorded, and the rate that priced a sale kept with it.

Piece-level stock

Serial-tracked pieces with weights, karat, stones, RFID tag and certificate. One tag, one piece, one history.

Pricing engine

Decimal arithmetic, gram, tola and ounce, karat or fineness, and a breakdown that always sums to its total.

Old-gold exchange

Melt loss, buy-back margin, valuation at the approved rate, an approval threshold you set, and re-issue to production.

Private clients

Standard to Royal tiers. Private records reach only the named relationship manager, the deputy and VIP managers, and every view is logged.

Owner command centre

Decision cards: what happened, what it is worth, what to do. Each opens the real record, under that person's own permissions.

Controls

The parts a gold business cannot get wrong

Enforced in the software, not left to a habit or a screen that hides a field.

A rate needs two people

The person who enters a rate cannot approve it. Neither can a manager working around the buttons: the state cannot be written directly, by anyone, including system code.

No price without an approved rate

If no rate is in force, the system refuses to price rather than showing a number someone might trust. Prices stay on the last approved rate until a new one is approved.

The payout is computed, never typed

An old-gold figure comes from the engine at the approved rate. Nobody at the counter can overwrite what a customer is owed.

Private clients stay private

Private and Royal records reach only the named manager, the deputy and VIP managers. Every view is logged, and automated outreach never goes to them.
