What the stylist may and may not do

Every price is read from the engine at the approved rate. It quotes; it never calculates.
It speaks only to a verified client about her own file — signed in by one-time code, and it sees only what she may see.
It cannot reserve, discount or take payment. "Ask Nadia to put them aside" creates a request for a person, not a hold on stock.
A person is always one tap away, and the whole thread goes with the handover.
