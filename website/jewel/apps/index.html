Jawhar is a set of apps on one engine. They share a client book, one approved gold rate and one set of permissions — so the counter, the stock book and the accounts are never telling you different things. Most of what you need is standard Odoo you configure, not software anyone has to write.

Why they fit together

Three things every app shares

One approved rate

No app prices anything from anywhere else. Change the rate and the counter, the stock book and the quotation all move together — or none of them do.

One client book

The till, the pipeline and the client app read the same record, under the same permissions. A Private client is invisible in all of them to anyone not named on her file.

One audit trail

Who entered, who approved, what it was worth, which rule applied. Including the refusals — an approval that was blocked is the control working.

Not sure which you need?

Most chains start with the gold rate, the client book and the pieces, and add the counter once the rate discipline is holding. We will tell you which of these is standard configuration and which is real build work, before you buy anything.
