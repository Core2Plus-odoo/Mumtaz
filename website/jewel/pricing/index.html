Questions you should ask

Why is there no per-user price?
Because in a shop everyone touches the system — the associate, the cashier, the manager, the workshop. Charging per head teaches staff to share a login, which destroys the audit trail. The audit trail is the product.
Do I pay for the Odoo apps as well?
No. Odoo Community is free and open source, and most of what a jewellery business needs is in it. You pay for the jewellery-specific parts: the rate discipline, the pricing engine, the piece model, the client book, the counter. If you ask us for something that is already standard, we will tell you, and we will configure it rather than sell it to you.
What if I only want one store?
Then the per-store lines are charged once, and the per-company lines are the bulk of it. The model is honest about that: a single shop pays proportionally more, because head-office capability costs the same whether it serves one counter or twenty.
Can I stop paying for an agent?
Yes, and that is deliberate. Each agent is subscribed separately so you can switch one off when it is not earning. Zayan is the exception — he coordinates the others, so he comes with any of them and is nothing on his own.
Where does my data live?
In your own Odoo database. The agents run beside it and reach it through authorised APIs, never by writing to the database directly — so if you remove them, the business keeps running exactly as before.
Is any of this actually finished?
Some of it. The gold rate with two-person approval, the pricing engine, the piece model, old gold and the client book are built and covered by 73 automated tests. The counter, Sales Hub and the agents are not. The apps page gives every app a real status, and we would rather you saw that before you saw a price.
