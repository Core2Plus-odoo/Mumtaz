Step 3 of 7

Who approves the gold rate

A rate is entered by one manager and approved by a different one. Until a second person approves it, nothing can be priced from it. This is enforced in the database, not in the screen, so it cannot be worked around later.

Usually the person who watches the market each morning.
A different person. Often the owner or the finance manager.

No override

There is no setting that lets one person enter and approve a rate. A one-person shop names a deputy — the accountant, a partner, a family member — as the second approver.

Workspace

Automation switched on

These run from day one. They are the scheduled actions that exist in the modules today — not a roadmap.
  • Occasion drafts — daily. Finds a client whose occasion falls inside its lead time, drafts the note and puts it on the relationship manager's list three days before. Nothing is sent automatically.
  • Pieces-on-approval reminders — daily. Any memo due back within two days raises an activity for the relationship manager, with the piece count and the insured value. Contact the client personally.
  • Owner decision cards — hourly. Rebuilds the cockpit: a rate waiting for a second person, an old-gold exchange over the threshold, metal exposure, an occasion coming, a memo overdue, stock sitting too long.
Beyond these three, automation is Odoo's own: server actions and no-code automation rules you write yourself under Settings → Technical. We configure them with you rather than hard-coding your process into the product.

What does not happen

Worth reading before you confirm.
  • No message goes to any client. Agents draft; a person sends.
  • No price exists until a second person approves a rate.
  • Private and Royal clients are invisible to anyone not named on their file, and are never messaged automatically.
  • Demo data, if you chose it, is synthetic. Do not add real clients to a demo workspace.
