Module 01 · Sales Hub

The client book, and everything that turns it into a sale.

Who your clients are, what they own, what they are likely to buy next, what is in the pipeline and what it is worth — and the counter where it all ends up. One module, built on Odoo 19 Community, designed for an associate with a client in front of them rather than for an ERP operator.

What's inside

Five parts, one module

Each tag says where it stands. Nothing marked in development or planned is available yet.

Client 360 Built

Tier, relationship manager and deputy, lifetime value, gold held. Pieces owned as photographs taken at handover. Taste, usual spend, and the occasions that matter. Compliance status beside it all.

Pipeline Standard Odoo

Enquiry, viewing booked, tray shared, reserved, won. Value and odds per stage, target against forecast, and why deals are lost — stock or price, not a guess.

The tray In development

Three to five pieces chosen for one client, each with its reasons and its live price. Shared as a branded link that expires and can be revoked. The client can ask to view; only staff reserve.

Outreach & upsell In development

Ranked next-piece suggestions with reasons. An outreach queue by channel, each resting on the consent that allows it, and a measure of what outreach is actually worth.

The counter In development

The client file sits on the till: tier, compliance, what she already owns. The piece prices from this morning's approved rate, old gold comes in at a computed credit, and the payment panel warns before a cash threshold is crossed.

Discretion Built

Private and Royal records reach only the named manager, the deputy and VIP managers. Opening one writes a line to an access log. Automated outbound never goes to them.

How it works

From a moment on the calendar to a piece in a box

The occasion comes upA wedding is three weeks out. A draft is prepared for the relationship manager — never sent automatically, and never to a Private or Royal client.
A tray is builtThree to five pieces for that client, in stock now, ranked with reasons and priced from the approved rate.
The client sees itShared as an expiring, revocable link. She likes two and asks about one. That lands back on her file, and the opportunity moves a stage.
A viewing is bookedThe piece goes out on approval with a return date and an insured value, or she comes in.
It closes at the counterHer file is on the till. The piece prices from this morning's rate, old gold comes in at a computed credit, and the sale checks itself against her compliance position.
The file updates itselfWhat she bought, what she was shown, who served her and how long it took — so the next conversation starts where this one ended.
Under it

Odoo 19 Community is the engine

No fork, no Enterprise licence. Stock, accounting, permissions and record rules stay where Odoo keeps them, which is why confidentiality and approvals are enforced in the database rather than in a screen. Being straight about what is standard is also how you can tell what you are actually paying us to build.

Standard Odoo, configured

crm for leads, stages, activities and forecasting — including predictive scoring, which Odoo computes from your own won and lost history. sale_management for quotations and orders. mail for the chatter, activities and followers on every record. point_of_sale for the till, offline working and a serial number on every line. loyalty when programmes are needed.

What we build on top

Client tiers and the confidentiality rules. The occasion calendar and pieces on approval. The deterministic ranking behind the tray. Consent per channel and per purpose. Gold-rate pricing carried onto an opportunity and a till line. And the agents, with the autonomy each one is allowed.
