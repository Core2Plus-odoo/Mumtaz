Autonomy

You set the ceiling. Per agent, per tenant, per action.

Every agent ships at A or B and goes no higher until someone configures it. The ceiling is enforced where the work happens, not in the prompt.

A
Observe

Read the data it is permitted to read, and analyse it.

B
Recommend

Say what it would do, and why, with the evidence attached.

C
Prepare

Draft the document, the entry, the message. Nothing leaves.

D
Execute

Carry out a permitted operation — only after a named person approves it.

E
Prohibited

Never. Approving a rate, crossing a tenant boundary, bypassing a control.

How they work together

One question, several specialists, one answer

Agents do not chat to each other freely. Every request carries a tenant, a user, the entity it concerns, the tools it may use and the approval it will need — as a validated structure, never as code.

A gold rate moves

  1. A verified rate arrives.
  2. Rayan analyses the move.
  3. Noor checks affected specifications.
  4. Amara assesses margin impact.
  5. Zayan consolidates it into one proposal.
  6. A person approves or rejects.
  7. Approved prices go through the pricing service. Everything is logged.

A retail opportunity

  1. Layla reads the client history she may see.
  2. She identifies the opportunity.
  3. Noor supplies the product knowledge.
  4. Rayan supplies the pricing context.
  5. Zayan consolidates the recommendation.
  6. A person reviews and sends.

A financial health review

  1. Zayan requests the assessment.
  2. Amara retrieves the authorised figures.
  3. She analyses cash flow, margin, expenses, balances.
  4. Zayan produces the report, with every source referenced.
  5. No entry is posted by anyone but a person.
Governance

The part that is not optional

These hold for every agent, on every plan. They are not features you subscribe to.

A number is read, never produced. Always the approved rate, through the pricing engine.
An agent runs as you. It cannot see a client, a store or a tenant that you cannot.
Private and Royal are capped. Their data never enters a model request; nothing is sent automatically.

Odoo stays the system of record

The agents run as independent services beside Odoo, not inside it. They never write to its database directly — every change goes through an authorised API, so Odoo's own permissions, approvals and audit trail still apply. Pull the agents out and the business keeps running.

Everything consequential is kept

Who asked, which agent answered, which tools it used, what evidence it cited, what it recommended, who approved it and what it cost in tokens. Retrieved documents, web content and uploads are treated as untrusted data — they can inform an answer, never change a permission.
